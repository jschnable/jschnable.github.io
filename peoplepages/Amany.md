Amany Gomaa completed her Ph.D. in Plant Pathology at UNL in May 2026, co-advised by Dr. Schnable and Dr. Garcia-Ruiz, studying plant–virus interactions using molecular and computational methods. She earned her B.S. and M.S. in Microbiology from Mansoura University, Egypt. She is currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at UNL and will join the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center as a postdoctoral researcher in January 2027.
