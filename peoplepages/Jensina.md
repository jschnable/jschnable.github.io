Jensina Davis completed her Ph.D. in the Complex Biosystems program at UNL, specializing in Integrated Plant Biology. She defended her dissertation in June 2026 and received her degree in mid-August 2026. She earned her B.S. in Agronomy and Seed Science with a minor in Statistics from Iowa State University in May 2022. Originally from Brookings, SD, she completed several internships with companies in the agriculture industry during her training and is passionate about advancing sustainability in agriculture using computational and statistical research methods.
