Danielle Leal Lamêgo is a Ph.D. candidate in Plant Biotechnology at the State University of Northern Rio de Janeiro (UENF), Brazil. Her research focuses on plant genetics and breeding, particularly the physiological, agronomic, and morphological responses of popcorn maize to drought stress. She is interested in understanding plant adaptation to water-limited environments through phenotyping, plant physiology, and quantitative data analysis. As a visiting researcher in the Schnable Lab at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, she is working on high-precision phenotyping of maize canopy architecture and the development of three-dimensional (3D) plant models. Her broader research interests include improving crop resilience and advancing phenotyping technologies to support plant breeding.
