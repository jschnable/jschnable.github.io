Danielle Leal Lamêgo is a Ph.D. candidate in Plant Biotechnology at the State University of Northern Rio de Janeiro (UENF), Brazil, where she studies the physiological, agronomic, and morphological responses of popcorn to drought stress. As a visiting researcher in the Schnable Lab, she is working on high-precision phenotyping of maize canopy architecture and the development of 3D plant models.
