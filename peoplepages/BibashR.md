Bibash Rokaya earned a B.S. in Agriculture from Tribhuvan University (IAAS) in Nepal and an M.S. in Agronomy from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, where he worked with Dr. Aaron Daigh on soil nitrogen mineralization kinetics and spatial variability across long-term tillage systems. He also worked as a Temporary Research Agronomist Assistant at Corteva Agriscience, supporting on-farm corn and soybean field trials, disease scouting, and UAV-based phenotyping. Bibash joined the Schnable Lab as a Research Specialist.
