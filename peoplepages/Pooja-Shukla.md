Pooja Shukla is a Fulbright Visiting Scholar from The Millet Lab at the University of Hyderabad in
India. Her research centers on the genomics of foxtail millet (*Setaria italica*), a C4 model crop.
She aims to systematically explore and use the crop's phenotypic and genetic diversity to identify
and characterize the genetic determinants underlying yield-contributing agronomic and nutritional
traits.

During her visit to the Schnable Lab, Pooja is constituting a core collection as a resource for
understanding the quantitative genetics of a USDA foxtail millet germplasm panel. She is using
genome-wide association studies to identify genetic determinants underlying architecture and
yield-related traits.
